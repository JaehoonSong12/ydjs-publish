\[

\]

By the Chain Rule, $\dfrac{d y}{d x}=\dfrac{d y}{d u} \cdot \dfrac{d u}{d x}=\sqrt{\sin u} \cdot 2 x$, where theorem (1) on page 250 is used to find $\dfrac{d y}{d u}$. Replace $u$ by $x^{2}$.
