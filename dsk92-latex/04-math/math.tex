In a right-angled triangle, the square of the hypotenuse is equal to the sum of the squares of the other two sides.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
Let \(a\) and \(b\) be the lengths of the two sides, and \(c\) the length of the hypotenuse. By the definition of a right-angled triangle, we have \(a^2 + b^2 = c^2\).
