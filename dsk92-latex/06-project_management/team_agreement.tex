\section{Purpose}
This agreement outlines the roles, responsibilities, and expectations of each team member involved in the project. By signing this agreement, all team members commit to working collaboratively and respectfully to ensure the successful completion of the project.

\subsection{Goals and Responsibilities}
The team will use Git and GitHub for version control, conduct code reviews before merging features, and divide tasks based on feature requirements and team members’ strengths. Weekly meetings will be held to review progress, and Asana will be used for tracking tasks. Team members should communicate regularly and contribute meaningfully to deliverables.

\section{Quality of Work}
Team members are expected to deliver high-quality work, prepared in advance of meetings. Each person is responsible for completing assigned tasks promptly, ensuring good communication regarding any difficulties or delays.

\subsection{Attendance and Participation}
Meetings are mandatory unless prior notice is given. Team members should arrive on time and remain present for the full duration. Contribution and active participation in discussions are essential.

\subsection{Decision-Making Process}
The team agrees to make decisions based on the following process: (1) Open discussions will be held to gather input from all team members. (2) A consensus-based approach will be prioritized, where possible. (3) If a consensus cannot be reached, decisions will be made by a majority vote.

\subsection{Conflict Resolution}
In the event of conflict or disagreement, the team will follow this process for resolution:
\begin{enumerate}
    \item Direct communication between the parties involved to resolve the issue.
    \item If unresolved, a neutral team member will mediate the discussion.
    \item As a last resort, the issue will be escalated to the project supervisor or a third-party mediator.
\end{enumerate}

\section{Communication and Meetings}
Timely communication is crucial for successful collaboration. Members should respond to messages within the same day (9am-5pm on weekdays) and within 30 minutes for urgent matters.
\begin{itemize}
    \item Regular meetings will be held on the following day(s) and time(s): in-person on Mon and Wed, online on MS Teams upon request.
    \item Communication will be maintained through the following platforms: MS Teams.
    \item Meeting minutes will be recorded automatically.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Emergency Situations}
In case of an emergency, the team member must notify the group as soon as possible. The team will meet to redistribute the affected work if necessary.

\subsection{Meeting Etiquette}
Team members must arrive on time and stay for the entire meeting unless excused. Minor lateness (under 5 minutes) is acceptable if it does not become a recurring issue. Leaving early is only acceptable if assigned tasks are completed and the group is informed.

\section{Task Management}
Each team member is responsible for completing tasks before the weekly meeting. Unfinished tasks must be communicated early to allow for adjustments. If a member fails to notify the team in a timely manner, their performance will be noted in peer feedback.

\subsection{Monitoring Contributions}
To keep track of individual contributions, a shared task-tracking spreadsheet will be used. Members will log their progress using GitHub pull requests, which will be reviewed during meetings. Peer evaluations will be conducted to assess each member’s contributions.

\section{Collaborative Work}
Edits to code or written content can be made when the member is 80\% certain of the necessity for improvement. Major changes should be discussed with the team before implementation, and all changes must be documented.

\subsection{Meeting Collaboration}
Meetings will be used to finalize collaborative parts of the project and review individual progress. Class time is a non-negotiable meeting time, and all team members are expected to attend and participate.
