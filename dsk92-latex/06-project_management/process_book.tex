\section*{Project Proposal}
\textbf{Music Trends and Insights: Visualization of Genres, Artists, and Listener Behaviors}
by Jaehoon Song (lead), Manya Jain, Devika Papal, Yashman P Singh, \textit{Georgia Institute of Technology} \\ \\ 
\textbf{Abstract:} This project explores various aspects of the music industry, focusing on how genres, artists, and listener demographics have evolved over time. Using a large dataset of music releases, listener behavior, and demographic information, the project aims to visualize trends in genre popularity across different regions and time periods. By tracking how certain genres rise or fall in popularity, the project seeks to uncover all the possible factors that drive these changes.\\ \\
Building on genre trends, the project will analyze how an artist's popularity evolves over time and identify the factors that contribute to their growth or decline. Additionally, the team will conduct an influence analysis, mapping how emerging artists are shaped by established ones through collaborations, covers, and remixes. This will provide insight into the flow of musical ideas and influence within the industry. To gain a deeper understanding of the audience, a demographic analysis will be performed, examining the age, gender, and regional preferences of listeners across genres and artists. Finally, the project will develop a dashboard that allows users to compare audio features, such as tempo and energy levels, in popular songs, helping them discover new music with similar attributes. \\ \\
By combining genre analysis, artist evolution, influence mapping, and demographic insights, the project will offer a comprehensive visualization of how music trends and preferences have shifted over time. \\ \\
\textbf{Data Source:} The primary data source for this project will be the Top 10,000 Spotify Songs dataset from Kaggle, which includes streaming counts, song attributes, and release years. Additional datasets, such as Top 100 Most Streamed Songs on Spotify and Music Genre Prediction, will enhance the analysis of trends in music consumption and genre classification.

\section*{Project Plan}
This project aims to analyze and visualize trends within the music industry, focusing on genres, artists, and listener behaviors over time. Using multiple datasets that cover Spotify streaming data, music features, and music sales by format, we will explore how these factors influence the popularity of music across different regions and demographics. The final output will be an interactive dashboard that allows users to explore these insights through visualizations.

\section*{Checklist:}
\begin{itemize}
  \item genre popularity
  \item artist popularity
  \item artist influence
  \item listener demographics
\end{itemize}

\section*{Audience}
We identified three potential audiences for our project:

\begin{itemize}
  \item Music Industry Professionals (Record labels, managers, marketing teams)
  \item Music Streaming Service Analysts (Spotify, Apple Music, Deezer teams)
  \item Music Enthusiasts/Researchers (Data analysts, academics interested in music trends)
\end{itemize}

After discussion, we selected Music Industry Professionals as our target audience. This audience is interested in understanding the factors behind the rise and fall of genres, artists, and listener behaviors, helping them make informed decisions on marketing strategies, talent scouting, and future music trends.

Target Audience Description: Our primary target audience includes music industry analysts, streaming platform users, and music enthusiasts. This group generally has a moderate to high level of visualization literacy and an interest in exploring trends across genres, artist evolution, and listener demographics.

\begin{itemize}
  \item Knowledge: Our audience has a strong understanding of the music industry and general trends in music consumption, but limited familiarity with technical data analysis.
  \item Interests: They are interested in tracking genre evolution, artist popularity, and regional listening behaviors to guide business strategies.
  \item Visualization Literacy: Basic understanding of data visualizations such as bar charts, line graphs, and heat maps. We will aim for simplicity in visual design but provide interactive elements to let them explore the data.
  \item Detail Level: The information will be presented at a high level but with the option to drill down into specific attributes (e.g., artist, genre, region) for deeper insights.
\end{itemize}

\section*{Audience Questions}
Here are 15 questions our target audience would likely want to explore based on our data:

\begin{enumerate}
  \item Which tracks have the highest popularity scores, and what genres do they represent?
  \item How does energy relate to popularity across different genres?
  \item What are the most popular genres from the past decade, and how have they evolved over time?
  \item How has the tempo (BPM) of top songs shifted over the years?
  \item Which artists have sustained popularity long-term, and how has their influence grown?
  \item Is explicit content more common in popular songs, and does this vary by region?
  \item How does a song's lyrical content impact its streaming performance across regions?
  \item What role does danceability play in determining a song's success in various countries?
  \item Which music sales formats (physical or digital) have grown the most, and how does this relate to genre?
  \item How do song features like positivity (valence) or loudness affect rankings on streaming platforms?
  \item What are the common features (danceability, energy, tempo) of the top-streamed songs worldwide?
  \item How does a track's speechiness influence its popularity in different markets?
  \item What trends exist in the "liveness" of songs, and how do live performance elements impact popularity by genre?
  \item Which regions favor specific genres, and how have regional preferences changed over time?
  \item What impact does playlist inclusion have on streaming success, and which songs are most featured in popular playlists?
\end{enumerate}

\section*{Data Collection}
This project aims to deliver a comprehensive analysis of music trends, providing valuable insights to music industry professionals. By focusing on the factors that influence song popularity, genre shifts, and artist success, we hope to give stakeholders the data-driven tools to predict and adapt to the evolving landscape of the music industry.

Each dataset attribute will be analyzed for type:

\begin{itemize}
  \item Genre (Categorical): Type of music.
  \item Artist (Categorical): Performing artist.
  \item Listeners by Demographic (Quantitative): Information on listener age, gender, and location.
\end{itemize}

We will be using the following datasets to address the questions mentioned above:

\begin{itemize}
  \item Top 10,000 Songs on Spotify (1950 - Now)
  \item Key attributes: Track Name, Artist Name, Popularity, Artist Genres, Danceability, Energy, Key, Tempo, Valence, Loudness, Album Release Date, Explicit flag, Duration, Instrumentalness, Liveness, and Speechiness.
  \item Data type: Mixed (categorical, ordinal, and quantitative)
  \item Source: Kaggle dataset featuring Spotify's most-streamed tracks from 1950 to the present.
  \item Spotify Most Streamed Songs
\end{itemize}

\textbf{Key attributes}: Track Name, Artist Name, Streams, Danceability \%, Energy \%, Acousticness \%, Instrumentalness \%, Speechiness \%, Liveness \%, Valence \%, Beats per Minute (BPM).

\begin{itemize}
  \item Data type: Quantitative and categorical
  \item Source: Kaggle dataset with detailed stream metrics for Spotify's top-streamed songs.
  \item Top 100 Most Streamed Songs on Spotify
  \item Key attributes: Track Name, Artist, Top Genre, Year, Beats per Minute (BPM), Energy \%, Danceability \%, Loudness (dB), Liveness \%, Valence \%, Length, Acousticness \%, Speechiness \%.
  \item Data type: Quantitative and categorical
  \item Source: Kaggle dataset featuring the 100 most-streamed Spotify tracks.
  \item Music Sales by Format and Year
  \item Key attributes: Format (physical, digital), Metric (units sold, revenue), Year, Sales Value.
  \item Data type: Categorical and quantitative
  \item Source: Dataset detailing music sales across physical and digital formats over 40 years.
\end{itemize}

\section*{Data Cleanup}
We will need to standardize date formats, ensure consistent naming conventions for genres and artists, and handle missing data, particularly for streaming metrics and song features.

\section*{Data Processing}
We plan to preprocess the data by:

\begin{itemize}
  \item Cleaning and normalizing genre and artist names
  \item Filling in missing or incomplete data (e.g., missing BPM, popularity scores)
  \item Merging datasets where artist and track names overlap
  \item Creating calculated fields like the total number of streams or average popularity per artist
  \item Filtering out irrelevant or duplicate records to focus on top artists and tracks.
\end{itemize}

\section*{Rising Insights}
The narrative builds through three key insights, all connected to cultural identity:

\begin{enumerate}
  \item Genre Trends Reflecting Culture: Visualizations will show the rise and fall of genre popularity over time and across regions. This provides a picture of how music preferences, like the increasing appeal of hip-hop or the resurgence of pop, reflect cultural values and collective identities. For example, hip-hop's global rise underscores a broader shift toward self-expression and social commentary as central themes in contemporary culture.
  \item Artist Influence and Cultural Exchange: By mapping artist influence, we reveal the way established musicians support emerging talent and genre blending through collaborations and remixes. This network represents a cultural exchange, where artists from different\\
backgrounds share and adapt styles, contributing to a shared musical identity and a culture of adaptability. Pop and hip-hop, for instance, showcase how genre crossover reflects an open, diverse cultural landscape.
  \item Demographic Insights and Identity: Analyzing demographics like age, gender, and region adds depth to how cultural identity shapes and is shaped by music preferences. Young listeners, for example, are shown gravitating toward newer genres, while older listeners often maintain loyalty to established genres. These patterns highlight the role of age and regional background in musical identity, revealing how music preferences connect to broader social identities.
\end{enumerate}

\section*{Main Message}
The core insight of the analysis is that music trends are a powerful reflection of cultural identity, highlighting how genres, artists, and listener demographics collectively capture cultural shifts. For instance, hip-hop's popularity signifies its role as a medium for identity and social expression, while genre blending speaks to a desire for diverse cultural experiences.

\section*{Implications of Trends in the Music Industry}
The story concludes by emphasizing how understanding music trends as reflections of cultural identity can help artists, producers, and platforms connect with audiences more deeply. By recognizing the cultural roots of genre and demographic trends, streaming platforms can refine recommendations to encourage exploration aligned with listeners' values and identities, creating a more personalized experience.
