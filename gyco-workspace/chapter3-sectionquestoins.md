## Chapter 3: Ratios, Rates, and Percents - Practice Questions

## Section 1: Ratio

### Short Question
**Q1.** There are 6 red marbles and 4 blue marbles in a bag. What is the ratio of red marbles to blue marbles?  
<details><summary>Answer</summary>  
**6:4**, which can be simplified to **3:2**.  
</details>

---

### Long Question
**Q2.** A class has 12 boys and 8 girls.  
a) Write the ratio of boys to girls in simplest form.  
b) Write the ratio of girls to total students in simplest form.  
<details><summary>Answer</summary>  
a) Boys to girls = **12:8**, simplified to **3:2**.  
b) Girls to total = \( 8:20 \), simplified to **2:5**.  
</details>

---

### Challenge Question
**Q3.** A recipe for bibimbap calls for 2 cups of rice and 3 cups of vegetables. If you want to make enough for twice as many people, keeping the same ratio, how many cups of rice and vegetables do you need?  
<details><summary>Answer</summary>  
Rice: \( 2 \times 2 = 4 \) cups  
Vegetables: \( 3 \times 2 = 6 \) cups  
</details>

---

## Section 2: Rates

### Short Question
**Q1.** A car travels 180 kilometers in 3 hours. What is its speed in km/h?  
<details><summary>Answer</summary>  
\( 180 \div 3 = 60 \) km/h  
</details>

---

### Long Question
**Q2.** A market sells 5 kg of rice for $20. What is the rate in dollars per kilogram?  
<details><summary>Answer</summary>  
\( 20 \div 5 = 4 \) → **$4 per kg**  
</details>

---

### Challenge Question
**Q3.** A Korean drama episode is 75 minutes long, and you watch 3 episodes in a day. How many minutes per day do you watch? Express as a rate in hours per day.  
<details><summary>Answer</summary>  
Total minutes: \( 75 \times 3 = 225 \) minutes  
Convert to hours: \( 225 \div 60 = 3.75 \) hours/day  
</details>

---

## Section 3: Unit Rate

### Short Question
**Q1.** If 4 notebooks cost $10, what is the cost per notebook?  
<details><summary>Answer</summary>  
\( 10 \div 4 = 2.50 \) → **$2.50 per notebook**  
</details>

---

### Long Question
**Q2.** A package of 12 bottles of water costs $6. Find the unit price per bottle.  
<details><summary>Answer</summary>  
\( 6 \div 12 = 0.50 \) → **$0.50 per bottle**  
</details>

---

### Challenge Question
**Q3.** At a Korean grocery store, a 2-pound bag of tangerines costs $5.50, and a 3-pound bag costs $7.20. Which is the better deal per pound?  
<details><summary>Answer</summary>  
2-pound bag: \( 5.50 \div 2 = 2.75 \) $/lb  
3-pound bag: \( 7.20 \div 3 = 2.40 \) $/lb  
**Better deal:** 3-pound bag ($2.40/lb)  
</details>

---

## Section 4: Proportions 

### Short Question
**Q1.** Is \( 2/5 = 8/20 \) a true proportion?  
<details><summary>Answer</summary>  
Yes. Both simplify to \( 2/5 \).  
</details>

---

### Long Question
**Q2.** If 3 cups of flour are needed to make 12 cookies, how many cups of flour are needed to make 20 cookies, assuming the recipe stays in proportion?  
<details><summary>Answer</summary>  
Set up: \( 3/12 = x/20 \)  
Cross-multiply: \( 12x = 60 \) → \( x = 5 \) cups  
</details>

---

### Challenge Question
**Q3.** A map uses a scale of 1 cm = 5 km. If two cities are 8.4 cm apart on the map, how far apart are they in real life?  
<details><summary>Answer</summary>  
\( 8.4 \times 5 = 42 \) km  
</details>

---

## Section 5: Percent 

### Short Question
**Q1.** Write 0.45 as a percent.  
<details><summary>Answer</summary>  
Move decimal two places right: \( 0.45 = 45\% \)  
</details>

---

### Long Question
**Q2.** A store is having a 25% off sale. If a jacket costs $60 before the sale, how much will it cost after the discount?  
<details><summary>Answer</summary>  
25% of $60 = \( 0.25 \times 60 = 15 \)  
Discounted price = \( 60 - 15 = 45 \)  
**Answer:** $45  
</details>

---

### Challenge Question
**Q3.** In a class of 40 students, 70% passed an exam. How many students passed, and how many failed?  
<details><summary>Answer</summary>  
Passed: \( 0.70 \times 40 = 28 \) students  
Failed: \( 40 - 28 = 12 \) students  
</details>
