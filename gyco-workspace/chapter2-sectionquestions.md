# Chapter 2: Variables and Algebra — Practice Questions

---

## Section 1: Variables and Algebraic Expressions (문자와 식)

### Short Question
**Q1. If \( x = 3 \), find the value of \( 2x + 7 \).**  
<details><summary>Answer</summary>  
\( 2(3) + 7 = 6 + 7 = \mathbf{13} \)  
</details>

---

### Long Question  
**Q2. Write an expression for “five less than triple a number y” and evaluate it when \( y = 4 \).**  
<details><summary>Answer</summary>  
Expression: \( 3y - 5 \)  
When \( y = 4 \): \( 3(4) - 5 = 12 - 5 = \mathbf{7} \)  
</details>

---

### Challenge Question  
**Q3. The cost of renting a bicycle is $8 plus $3 per hour. Write an expression for the total cost if you rent it for \( h \) hours. Find the cost if you rent it for 5 hours.**  
<details><summary>Answer</summary>  
Expression: \( 8 + 3h \)  
If \( h = 5 \): \( 8 + 3(5) = 8 + 15 = \mathbf{23} \) dollars  
</details>

## Section 2: Simplifying Expressions (식의 간단화)

### Short Question  
**Q1. Simplify: \( 7x + 5x \)**  
<details><summary>Answer</summary>  
\(\mathbf{12x}\)  
</details>

---

### Long Question  
**Q2. Use the distributive property to simplify \( 4(a + 6) \).**  
<details><summary>Answer</summary>  
\( 4a + 24 \)  
</details>

---

### Challenge Question  
**Q3. Simplify: \( 3x + 7 - 2x + 4 \)**  
<details><summary>Answer</summary>  
Combine like terms: \( (3x - 2x) + (7 + 4) = x + 11 \)  
</details>

## Section 4: Solving Multi-Step Equations (여러 단계 방정식)

### Short Question  
**Q1. Solve: \( 2x + 5 = 13 \)**  
<details><summary>Answer</summary>  
\( 2x = 8 \) → \( x = 4 \)  
</details>

---

### Long Question  
**Q2. Solve: \( 3(x - 2) = 15 \)**  
<details><summary>Answer</summary>  
\( x - 2 = 5 \) → \( x = 7 \)  
</details>

---

### Challenge Question  
**Q3. Solve: \( 4x + 7 = 3x + 12 \)**  
<details><summary>Answer</summary>  
Subtract \( 3x \): \( x + 7 = 12 \)  
Subtract 7: \( x = 5 \)  
</details>

---

## Section 5: Inequalities (부등식)

### Short Question  
**Q1. Solve: \( x + 4 < 10 \)**  
<details><summary>Answer</summary>  
\( x < 6 \)  
</details>

---

### Long Question  
**Q2. Solve and graph: \( -3x \geq 12 \)**  
<details><summary>Answer</summary>  
Divide by \(-3\) and flip the sign: \( x \leq -4 \)  
Graph: closed circle at –4, shade to the left.  
</details>

---

### Challenge Question  
**Q3. A movie ticket costs $12. Write and solve an inequality for the number of tickets you can buy with $80.**  
<details><summary>Answer</summary>  
\( 12t \leq 80 \)  
\( t \leq 6.\overline{6} \) → You can buy at most **6 tickets**.  
</details>
