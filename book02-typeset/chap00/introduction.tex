\section*{Introduction}
Welcome to this math book designed especially for Korean students learning English! 
This book is organized to help you build a strong foundation in mathematics while 
improving your English skills. Each section covers a key topic, starting with clear 
explanations and followed by examples, practice questions, and helpful tips.

The book is divided into major sections such as Numbers \& Operations, Expressions 
\& Equations, Geometry, 
Data \& Statistics, and more. Within each section, you will find step-by-step guides, 
vocabulary lists, and real-world applications. Special features include bilingual 
glossaries, common mistakes to avoid, and mini quizzes to check your understanding.

To get the most out of this book, we recommend reading each section carefully, 
trying the examples on your own, and using the vocabulary lists to strengthen 
your math English. Do not hesitate to revisit earlier sections if you need a 
refresher. Remember, learning math in a new language takes time and practice—be 
patient with yourself and celebrate your progress!

We hope this book becomes a valuable companion on your journey to mastering math 
and English together.
