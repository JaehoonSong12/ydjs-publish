\begin{letter}{To the Admissions Committee \\ Hampshire College Summer Studies in Mathematics (HCSSiM)}

\opening{Dear Admissions Committee,}

\textbf{Re:} Letter of Recommendation for Danny Lee

It is my pleasure to recommend Danny Lee for admission to the Hampshire College Summer Studies in Mathematics (HCSSiM).
As Danny's tutor in algebra and calculus, I have seen him move quickly through the material while maturing
from a consistently strong student into someone who pursues mathematics with independence, care, and sustained curiosity.

Danny moved through the standard curriculum, including Calculus BC, at an unusually fast pace.
Where many students are satisfied with the procedural side of differentiation and integration,
Danny kept asking for the ``why'' behind the theorems. Our sessions regularly extended beyond
the usual scope of a high school course so we could examine the reasoning that makes the techniques work.

The clearest illustration of Danny's mathematical maturity came during our study of sequences and series.
Rather than memorizing convergence tests as recipes, he wanted to understand how convergence is decided
in principle. I introduced him to ideas from real analysis, including the precise $\epsilon$-definition
of a limit. He absorbed the abstraction quickly and responded with formal proofs of the theorems.

Comfort at that level, well beyond typical prerequisites, suggests the intellectual ability and genuine
curiosity that an intensive program like HCSSiM requires. Danny does not shy away from difficult problems,
he meets them with patience, discipline, and an appetite for depth.

I recommend him without reservation, and I am confident he will contribute thoughtfully to your
mathematical community this summer.

\closing{Sincerely,}

\end{letter}
