# Word Problems in English with Korean Hints (영어로 된 문제 + 한국어 힌트)

**1.**  
**English:** Maria has 3 fewer than twice as many apples as Tom. If Tom has x apples, how many does Maria have?  
**Hint (Korean):** 마리아는 톰보다 3개 적고, 톰의 사과 수의 2배라고 했어요. 식을 만들어 보세요.  
**Answer:** $2x - 3$

---

**2.**  
**English:** The temperature dropped 5 degrees below 0. What number represents the temperature now?  
**Hint (Korean):** 0도에서 5도 떨어졌다면?  
**Answer:** $-5$

---

**3.**  
**English:** A box weighs 4.5 kg. What is the total weight of 10 boxes?  
**Hint (Korean):** 상자 하나는 4.5kg, 열 개는? 곱하세요.  
**Answer:** $4.5 × 10 = 45$

---

**4.**  
**English:** A scientist wrote a number as $3.2 × 10^4$. What is this number in standard form?  
**Hint (Korean):** 소수점 오른쪽으로 4칸 움직이세요.  
**Answer:** $32,000$

---

**5.**  
**English:** Jin has $x$ dollars. After spending $12, he has $20 left. What is the equation?  
**Hint (Korean):** x에서 12를 빼면 20이래요.  
**Answer:** $x - 12 = 20$

# Common Mistakes Korean Students Make (한국 학생들이 자주 하는 실수)

### 1. Confusing Signs  
* Mistake: $-3 + 5 = -8$  
* Correction: $-3 + 5 = 2$  
**Tip:** Use number lines to add and subtract with negatives.

---

### 2. Forgetting to Flip the Inequality  
* Mistake: $-2x < 6$ → $x < -3$  
* Correction: Flip the sign → $x > -3$  
**Tip:** Always flip the sign when dividing by a negative number.

---

### 3. Misreading Exponents  
* Mistake: $2^3 = 6$  
* Correction: $2^3 = 2 × 2 × 2 = 8$  
**Tip:** Multiply repeatedly, not base × exponent.

---

### 4. Combining Unlike Terms  
* Mistake: $2x + 3 = 5x$  
* Correction: $2x + 3$ cannot combine with $5x$  
**Tip:** Combine only like terms (same variable and power).

---

### 5. Forgetting Parentheses in Distribution  
* Mistake: $3(x + 2) = 3x + 2$  
* Correction: $3(x + 2) = 3x + 6$  
**Tip:** Distribute to **every** term inside parentheses.
