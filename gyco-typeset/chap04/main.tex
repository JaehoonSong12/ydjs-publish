\chapter{Geometry}
\kr{(기하)}

This chapter introduces the basic ideas of geometry in a clear, friendly way. 
You will learn about points, lines, line segments, and rays; types of angles 
and how to measure them; simple 2D shapes (triangles, quadrilaterals, circles) 
and their properties; area, perimeter, surface area, and volume; and the 
coordinate plane and simple transformations.

Geometry is the study of shapes, sizes, and positions in space. It helps us 
understand the world around us, from the design of buildings to the patterns 
in nature. Each section uses short paragraphs and lists to make the ideas 
easy to read and practice. You'll discover how to measure, classify, and 
transform shapes using simple tools and clear rules.
