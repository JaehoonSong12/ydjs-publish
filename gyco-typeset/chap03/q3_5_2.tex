\subsection{Long Question (Answer)}
A store is having a 25\% off sale. If a jacket costs \$60 before the sale, how much will it cost after the discount?

\textbf{Answer:}\\
25\% of \$60 = $0.25 \times 60 = 15$\\
Discounted price = $60 - 15 = 45$\\
\textbf{Answer:} \$45
