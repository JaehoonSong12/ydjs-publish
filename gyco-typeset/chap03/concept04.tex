\section{Proportions}

A \textbf{proportion} is a statement that shows two ratios or rates are \textbf{equal}. It helps us solve problems where quantities grow or shrink at the same rate.

**Example:**  
If $2/3 = 4/6$, this is a proportion because both sides are equal.

\subsection*{How to Solve Proportions}

Use \textbf{cross multiplication}:
\begin{itemize}
  \item $2/3 = 4/6$ → $2 × 6 = 3 × 4$
  \item $12 = 12$ ✅ This means it is a true proportion.
\end{itemize}

You can also use proportions to find unknown numbers:

**Example:**
\[
\frac{x}{5} = \frac{6}{10} \Rightarrow 10x = 30 \Rightarrow x = 3
\]

\subsection*{Everyday Examples}

\begin{itemize}
  \item A map says 1 inch = 50 miles → This ratio helps calculate real distances.
  \item A recipe that serves 2 people can be doubled to serve 4 using proportions.
\end{itemize}

Proportions are used in architecture, photography, and fashion design to maintain balance and scale!

\subsection{Practice Questions}

\begin{enumerate}
  \item \textbf{Short Question} \\
  Solve the proportion: $\frac{3}{4} = \frac{x}{12}$
  
  \textbf{Answer:} \textbf{x = 9} — Cross multiply: $3 \times 12 = 4x$, so $36 = 4x$, then $x = 9$.

  \item \textbf{Long Question} \\
  If 2 cups of flour make 12 cookies, how many cups of flour are needed to make 36 cookies?
  
  \textbf{Answer:} \\
  Set up proportion: $\frac{2 \text{ cups}}{12 \text{ cookies}} = \frac{x \text{ cups}}{36 \text{ cookies}}$ \\
  Cross multiply: $2 \times 36 = 12x$ \\
  $72 = 12x$ \\
  $x = 6$ \\
  \textbf{Answer:} 6 cups of flour

  \item \textbf{Challenge Question} \\
  On a map, 1 inch represents 25 miles. If two cities are 3.5 inches apart on the map, how far apart are they in real life?
  
  \textbf{Answer:} \\
  Set up proportion: $\frac{1 \text{ inch}}{25 \text{ miles}} = \frac{3.5 \text{ inches}}{x \text{ miles}}$ \\
  Cross multiply: $1 \times x = 25 \times 3.5$ \\
  $x = 87.5$ \\
  \textbf{Answer:} 87.5 miles
\end{enumerate}
