\chapter{Ratios, Rates, and Percents \kr{(비율, 속도, 백분율)}}

Welcome to Unit 3! In this chapter, you'll learn how to compare amounts using \emph{ratios}, measure how fast things change with \emph{rates}, and work with \emph{percents} in real-life situations. These ideas are not just math—they show up in shopping, cooking, sports, and money. Ratios, rates, and percents help us describe relationships between numbers in clear and simple ways.

Ratios help us compare two quantities, like the number of boys to girls in a class or the ingredients in a recipe. Rates measure how things change over time or distance, such as speed in miles per hour or price per item. Percents give us a way to express parts of a whole, making it easy to understand discounts, grades, and statistics.
