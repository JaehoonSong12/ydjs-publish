\subsection*{Challenge Question (Answer)}
In a class of 40 students, 70\% passed an exam. How many students passed, and how many failed?

\textbf{Answer:}\\
Passed: $0.70 \times 40 = 28$ students\\
Failed: $40 - 28 = 12$ students
