\subsection{Long Question (Answer)}

\textbf{Answer:}\\
Convert to decimals:\\
- 0.199\\
- $\frac{1}{3} \approx 0.333$\\
- 0.25\\
- 0.2\\
\textbf{Ordered:} 0.199 < 0.2 < 0.25 < $\frac{1}{3}$
