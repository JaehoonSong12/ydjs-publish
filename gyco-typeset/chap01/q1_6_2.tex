\subsection{Long Question (Answer)}
Find the LCM of 6 and 10 using prime factorization. Show your steps.

\textbf{Answer:}\\
- 6 = 2 × 3\\
- 10 = 2 × 5\\
Take the highest powers: 2 × 3 × 5 = \textbf{30}
