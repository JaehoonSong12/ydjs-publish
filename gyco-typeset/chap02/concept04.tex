\section{Solving Multi-Step Equations}

In this section, we'll learn how to solve equations with more than one operation. We'll use combining like terms and distribution before solving. First, \textbf{simplify both sides} of the equation using distribution and combining like terms. Then, \textbf{isolate the variable} step by step using inverse operations.

Solving multi-step equations builds confidence and logical thinking. You break down a big problem into smaller pieces—just like solving a mystery one clue at a time. People use multi-step equations in budgeting, engineering, and even cooking recipes when they need to scale quantities.

\subsection*{Example: Multi-Step Problem Solving}

Let's solve the equation $2(x + 3) = 14$ step by step:

\textbf{Step 1:} Distribute
\[
2(x + 3) = 14 \quad \rightarrow \quad 2x + 6 = 14
\]

\textbf{Step 2:} Subtract 6 from both sides
\[
2x + 6 - 6 = 14 - 6 \quad \rightarrow \quad 2x = 8
\]

\textbf{Step 3:} Divide both sides by 2
\[
\frac{2x}{2} = \frac{8}{2} \quad \rightarrow \quad x = 4
\]

Don't rush—write each step clearly on its own line. Make sure each side of the equation is simplified before you solve. Always check your solution by plugging it back into the original equation.

\subsection{Practice Questions}

\begin{enumerate}
  \item \textbf{Short Question} \\
  Solve: $3x + 5 = 20$
  
  \textbf{Answer:} \textbf{x = 5} — Subtract 5: $3x = 15$, then divide by 3: $x = 5$.

  \item \textbf{Long Question} \\
  Solve: $2(x - 3) = 10$. Show each step clearly.
  
  \textbf{Answer:} \\
  Step 1: Distribute: $2x - 6 = 10$ \\
  Step 2: Add 6: $2x = 16$ \\
  Step 3: Divide by 2: $x = 8$ \\
  Check: $2(8 - 3) = 2(5) = 10$ ✓ \\
  \textbf{Answer:} $x = 8$

  \item \textbf{Challenge Question} \\
  Solve: $3(x + 2) - 2(x - 1) = 15$. Show all steps and check your answer.
  
  \textbf{Answer:} \\
  Step 1: Distribute: $3x + 6 - 2x + 2 = 15$ \\
  Step 2: Combine like terms: $x + 8 = 15$ \\
  Step 3: Subtract 8: $x = 7$ \\
  Check: $3(7 + 2) - 2(7 - 1) = 3(9) - 2(6) = 27 - 12 = 15$ ✓ \\
  \textbf{Answer:} $x = 7$
\end{enumerate}
