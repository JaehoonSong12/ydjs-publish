\subsection{Long Question (Answer)}
Write an expression for "five less than triple a number y" and evaluate it when $y = 4$.

\textbf{Answer:}\\
Expression: $3y - 5$\\
When $y = 4$: $3(4) - 5 = 12 - 5 = \mathbf{7}$
