\subsection{Challenge Question (Answer)}
A movie ticket costs \$12. Write and solve an inequality for the number of tickets you can buy with \$80.

\textbf{Answer:}\\
$12t \leq 80$\\
$t \leq 6.\overline{6}$ → You can buy at most \textbf{6 tickets}.
