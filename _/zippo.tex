\section*{Product Categories}
The Zippo Classic Collection offers a comprehensive range of 
windproof lighters, accessories, and hand warmers. Each product 
is built to Zippo's exacting standards with lifetime guarantees 
and Made-in-USA quality.
\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{Windproof Lighters} -- Iconic metal lighters with 
  lifetime guarantee and windproof design.
    \begin{itemize}
      \item \href{https://zippo.com/products/classic-brushed-chrome}{\textbf{Classic Brushed Chrome}} -- Timeless 
      design with brushed chrome finish that resists fingerprints and scratches. Features brass construction 
      with chrome plating and standard wick-and-flint ignition. 
      \item \href{https://zippo.com/products/classic-street-chrome}{\textbf{Classic Street Chrome™}} -- Distressed 
      finish that develops character over time. Each case develops a unique patina while maintaining full Zippo 
      reliability. Perfect for collectors seeking a vintage, lived-in aesthetic.
      \item \href{https://zippo.com/products/armor-high-polish-chrome}{\textbf{Armor® High Polish Chrome}} -- Heavier-duty 
      case with 1.5 times thick walls for enhanced durability. Mirror-like high polish finish resists tarnishing and corrosion. 
      Ideal for users seeking a robust, long-lasting lighter.
    \end{itemize}
  \item \textbf{Fuel} -- High-quality lighter fluids and fuel canisters for optimal performance.
    \begin{itemize}
      \item \href{https://zippo.com/collections/lighter-fuel}{\textbf{Lighter Fuel}} -- Low-odor, high-purity 
      naphtha-based fluid for Zippo lighters. Provides consistent, clean-burning performance and extends wick 
      life. Store in cool, ventilated areas away from heat sources.
      \item \href{https://zippo.com/products/fuel-canister}{\textbf{Fuel Canister}} -- Sealed canisters for 
      Zippo hand warmers and compatible devices. Convenient single or multi-use options with leak-resistant 
      packaging. Use only for intended devices and dispose responsibly.
    \end{itemize}
  \item \textbf{Inserts} -- Interchangeable modules for specialized applications and easy maintenance.
    \begin{itemize}
      \item \href{https://zippo.com/collections/flints-wicks}{\textbf{Standard Wick/Flint Insert}} -- Original 
      insert assembly with wick, chimney, cotton packing, and flint tube. Easy one-handed replacement restores 
      optimal flame height and reliability. Keep spare flints and wicks on hand.
      \item \href{https://zippo.com/products/pipe-insert}{\textbf{Pipe Insert}} -- Specialized low-flame insert 
      for pipe smokers. Features narrower chimney and modified wick arrangement for controlled, directed flame. 
      Reversible installation with cooler case temperatures and improved fuel economy.
    \end{itemize}
  \item \textbf{Pouches \& Displays} -- Protective storage solutions for safe transport and display.
    \begin{itemize}
      \item \href{https://zippo.com/products/tactical-pouch}{\textbf{Tactical Pouch}} -- Heavy-duty Made-in-USA 
      protective pouch with abrasion-resistant fabric and reinforced seams. Belt or pack mounting with secure 
      closure. Includes space for spare flints and maintenance tools.
    \end{itemize}
  \item \textbf{Zippo Flasks} -- Durable stainless steel flasks for carrying spirits.
    \begin{itemize}
      \item \href{https://zippo.com/products/zippo-flask-lighter-gift-set-new}{\textbf{3 oz Stainless Steel Flask}} -- 
      Sleek, compact design with brushed stainless steel finish. Leak-proof screw cap and contoured shape for 
      comfortable carry. Ideal for outdoor adventures and social events.
    \end{itemize}
  \item \textbf{Hand Warmers} -- Refillable portable heating solutions for outdoor activities.
    \begin{itemize}
      \item \href{https://zippo.com/collections/refillable}{\textbf{6H/12H-Refillable}} -- Compact 
      hand warmer providing consistent heat. Includes replaceable burner and protective pouch. 
      Ideal for day trips and outdoor events.
      \item \href{https://zippo.com/products/refillable-hand-warmer-replacement-burner}{\textbf{Replacement Burner}} -- Service 
      part for regular hand warmer maintenance. Replaces fuel delivery and ignition components. 
    \end{itemize}
\end{itemize}
